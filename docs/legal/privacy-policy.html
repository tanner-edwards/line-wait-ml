Club 32 Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

Club 32 ("we," "our," "the app") helps you decide what to do next at Disneyland Resort by predicting how ride wait times will change throughout the day. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information We Collect

DataWhy we collect it
Precise location To calculate walk time to rides and tailor recommendations to where you are in the park. Sent to our server only while the app is open to generate your current recommendations — it is not used to track you outside the app.
Email address Created when you sign in (including via Sign in with Apple), used to identify your account and link your trip purchase or promo code to it.
Account/device identifiers A unique account ID and a push notification token, used to keep your session signed in and to deliver ride alerts you've opted into.
Purchase history Records of trip-access purchases (processed by Apple) or promo codes redeemed, used to unlock the app's full features for your trip dates.

We do not collect analytics, advertising, or diagnostic data, and we do not use any third-party tracking or ad networks. Location and account data are used only to run the app's features — never sold, and never shared for advertising purposes.

How Information Is Shared

  • Apple processes all in-app purchases directly; we never see your payment details.
  • Firebase (Google Cloud) stores your account and trip records as our infrastructure provider, under their standard data processing terms.
  • We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Your Choices

  • Location access can be turned off any time in iOS Settings → Privacy → Location Services. The app will still work, but walk-time and location-based recommendations won't be available.
  • Notifications can be disabled in iOS Settings → Notifications.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us below.

Data Retention

We retain account and trip data for as long as your account is active, or until you request deletion. Location data sent with a request is used to generate that request's response and is not retained as part of your permanent account record.

Children's Privacy

Club 32 is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at support.club32@gmail.com.
